## Purpose
This file documents the materials reviewed and the decisions made before beginning to collect auction data from the MAC.BID API.

### Terms and Conditions
Data collection policies are defined in MAC.BID's official terms and conditions, which can be accessed [here](./evidence/2026-10-07/terms-of-use.pdf).

The relevant portion can be found in section 3, where it states:

*“Users undertake that information concerning other Users ... obtained through the Site shall not be used ... for any purpose other than participation in auctions or use of the Site.”*

### robots.txt
Web crawling policies may be found in MAC.BID's `robots.txt` file, as documented [here](./evidence/2026-10-07/robots.txt).

As of October 7, 2026, no web crawling limitations are in place within `robots.txt`.
